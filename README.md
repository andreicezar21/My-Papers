## Papers

### A Probabilistic Analysis and Optimization of a Casino Game  
**December 2024**

A rigorous construction and optimization of a casino game using probability theory and
measure theory. The paper derives the full probability distribution of outcomes, designs
payout functions under profitability constraints, and validates results using experimental
data.

### Analysis of Spousal Homicide Rates in Ontario and Quebec  
**September 2024**

A comparative statistical analysis of spousal homicide rates in Ontario and Quebec using
Statistics Canada data (1997–2023). Includes numerical summaries, box plots, histograms,
time series analysis, and normality testing.

📄 **PDF:**  
[View Paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hy1-lhwrc2M4Mf8eSLih1UU2zcF4wECZ/view)

### Investigation of Correlation Between Spousal Homicide Rates and Divorce Rates  
**November 2024**

An investigation into the relationship between divorce rates and spousal homicide rates
in Ontario. The paper performs correlation analysis, linear regression, residual analysis,
and examines the impact of outliers on model strength.
